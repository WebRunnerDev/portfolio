Throwaway prototypes of the home page. Each one is a different big idea; pick by eye, then it becomes tickets.

  2. BVelocity · РазгонA run through a tunnel of diamond frames; scroll speed is running speed. Keeps today's rules.
  3. CPrism · ПризмаA glass diamond refracts the brand and shatters into the seven logo diamonds. Breaks “not a studio's look”.
  4. DWeb · ПаутинаA's cast blocks become nodes of a web of molten threads; a hot crawler runs it, the web trembles under the pointer.
  5. EIndex · МатрицаA's cast metal, but the brand itself is poured into letter moulds over a slab engraved with a matrix of the web: URLs, requests, status codes, read by a hot crawler.
